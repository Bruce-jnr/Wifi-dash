# Refund Policy

**Last Updated:** July 22, 2026

## Overview

Thank you for choosing **[Your Company Name]**.

We strive to provide reliable Wi-Fi internet services. This Refund Policy explains the circumstances under which refunds may be issued.

---

## Subscription Payments

Payments made for Wi-Fi subscriptions activate your internet service immediately or within the stated activation period.

Because internet access is a digital service, refunds are generally not available once the service has been activated and used.

---

## Eligible Refunds

A refund may be considered if:

- You were charged more than once for the same transaction.
- Payment was successful but your subscription was never activated due to a system error.
- You were charged after a confirmed service cancellation.
- There was an error caused by our billing system.

Refund requests are reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

---

## Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will generally not be provided for:

- Change of mind after purchase.
- Failure to use the purchased internet service.
- Incorrect package selection by the customer.
- Temporary service interruptions caused by maintenance or factors beyond our control.
- Expired subscriptions.
- Customer device or network configuration issues.

---

## Duplicate Payments

If you accidentally make duplicate payments, please contact us as soon as possible.

Once verified, the duplicate payment will be refunded using the original payment method where possible.

---

## Failed or Pending Payments

If your payment fails or remains pending, no refund is required because funds are generally not captured.

If your account is debited but your subscription is not activated, contact our support team with your transaction reference.

---

## Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed through the original payment method.

Depending on your payment provider or financial institution, refunds may take between **5 and 15 business days** to appear.

---

## How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please provide:

- Full name
- Registered phone number
- Email address
- Paystack transaction reference
- Date of payment
- Amount paid
- Reason for the refund request

---

## Fraud Prevention

We reserve the right to refuse refund requests where fraud, abuse, or misuse of our services is suspected.

---

## Service Availability

While we strive for uninterrupted service, occasional maintenance, upgrades, or unforeseen outages may occur. Such events do not automatically qualify for a refund unless required by applicable law.

---

## Policy Changes

We reserve the right to modify this Refund Policy at any time. Updates become effective immediately upon publication on our website.
